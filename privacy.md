# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 18 October 2026*

This policy explains how **Reading Lamp (Đèn Đọc)** (the "App"), developed by **[DEVELOPER NAME]** ("we"), handles your information.

## 1. Summary

- The App has **no accounts** and **does not ask for your name, email or phone number**.
- Links you paste, page text, your library, listening history and settings **stay on your device**. We do not collect them.
- The App shows **Google AdMob ads**. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show relevant ads **if you allow it**.
- Subscriptions are processed by **Apple** or **Google**. We never receive your card details.

## 2. Data processed on your device

| Data | Purpose | Storage |
|---|---|---|
| Links of pages you open | Load and read the page aloud | On device |
| Page text (ads and navigation removed) | Read aloud, save for offline listening | On device; offline chapters **auto-delete after 30 days** |
| Library, position, history | Resume where you left off | On device |
| Voice settings (voice, speed, pauses) | Personalise reading | On device |
| Listening time today | Count the free daily listening time | On device, resets daily |

When you open a link, the App **connects directly to that website**, like a browser does. That website may log your IP address and device information under its own policy. We do not control third-party websites.

## 3. Voices

The App uses the **operating system's built-in text-to-speech** (Apple on iOS; Google or the device maker on Android).

- Voices marked **"Network"** on Android send the text being read to the voice provider's servers (usually Google) to produce audio, under that provider's policy. Choose an **Offline** voice if you prefer.
- All other voices run entirely on your device.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free because of ads. AdMob may collect:

- Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android);
- IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device type, OS version;
- Ad interaction and diagnostic data.

This is used to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. **Personalised ads are only enabled with your permission:**

- **iOS:** through the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Change it anytime in *Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking*.
- **Android:** delete or reset your advertising ID in *Settings › Google › Ads* (menu names vary by device).
- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** you will see Google's consent form and can reopen it in the App under *Settings › Ad privacy*.

If you decline, the App keeps all its features; ads are simply less relevant. How Google uses data: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

The App **does not send the links or text you listen to** to any ad network.

## 5. Purchases

Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The App only receives your **subscription status** to remove ads and limits. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

## 6. Remote configuration

The App periodically downloads a **public configuration file** (for example, a list of unsupported websites) from GitHub. GitHub may log your IP address under GitHub's policy. The file contains none of your data and nothing is uploaded.

## 7. Children

The App is **not directed to children under 13** (under 16 in the EEA). Because it can open any website, it carries an appropriate age rating in the stores. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

## 8. Retention and deletion

- On-device data stays until you delete it in *Settings › Delete saved chapters / Clear listening history*, or uninstall the App.
- We keep no server-side data about you, so there is no account to delete.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law (including Vietnam's Decree 13/2023/ND-CP and the GDPR), you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete your data and withdraw consent. Most data is on your device, so you can exercise these rights in the App. For advertising data, use the options in section 4 or contact Google.

## 10. Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date when this policy changes, and notify you in the App of significant changes.
